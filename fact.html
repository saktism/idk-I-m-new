Here are some interesting facts about Football!

Did you know?

The "bandage" or patch frequently worn by Italian football legend Gennaro Gattuso on his eye is a medical cover used to manage ocular myasthenia. This is an autoimmune neuromuscular disease that affects the extraocular muscles controlling eye and eyelid movement

Son Heung-min learned to speak German while playing in the Bundesliga by watching episodes of SpongeBob SquarePants

Olympique Lyonnais holds the all-time French football record of winning seven consecutive Ligue 1 titles from the 2001/02 through the 2007/08 seasons.
